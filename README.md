  
# Adhi's PCB Project Portofolio.

  

This Portofolio are showcase for my previous work in PCB design and fabrication using both EASYEDA , Altium and KiCAD(most of the projected created using EASYEDA and KiCAD), and each project are separated to it's own folder for more detailed explainations.

  
  

## Overview

  

I am a Mid-Level embedded systems engineer with experience in designing and fabricating PCBs using EasyEDA and Altium. I have designed and fabricated numerous PCBs for various applications, including Digital to Analog Converter, Hi-Fi Amplifier, Power Supply ,RF Cascoding Amplifier.

## Projects

* Portable Headphone Amplifier
* Regulated Capacitance Multipler

## Skills
-   **EDA Tools & Software**: Advanced proficiency in **Altium Designer** (complex multi-layer designs) and **EasyEDA** (rapid prototyping and agile layouts); ECAD/MCAD integration using **STEP 3D models** and **Altium CoDesigner**.
-   **PCB Layout & Architecture**: Rigid and flexible **multi-layer stackup design**, strategic mixed-signal partitioning, controlled impedance calculations, and **high-speed digital routing** (DDR3/DDR4, PCIe, USB 3.0, HDMI).
-   **Simulation & Signal Integrity**: Pre- and post-layout **Signal Integrity (SI)**, **Power Integrity (PI)**, and thermal analysis utilizing **PSpice** and **LTspice** to mitigate cross-talk and voltage drops.
-   **DFM, DFT & Manufacturing**: Deep implementation of Design for Manufacturability (**DFM**) and Design for Test (**DFT**) workflows; generation of production-ready manufacturing files including **Gerber (RS-274X/X2)**, **Excellon drill files**, and IPC-D-356 netlists.
-   **Standards & Compliance**: Strict adherence to **IPC standards** (IPC-2221/610), **GD&T**, and foundational design methodologies for **EMI/EMC mitigation** to meet FCC/CE regulatory guidelines.
-   **Component & Supply Chain Management**: **Custom footprint/symbol creation** according to IPC specs; proactive **BOM optimization**, component selection, and lifecycle tracking to mitigate obsolescence using SiliconExpert/Octopart.
-   **Hardware Testing & Assembly**: Hands-on **SMT and through-hole assembly**, precision soldering, and hardware debugging using laboratory instruments including **oscilloscopes**, **DMMs**, and spectrum analyzers.
